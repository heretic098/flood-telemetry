🌧️ Raw Liquid Depth Only (No Flow Speculation)

Environment Agency tipping-bucket raingauges ? report raw liquid rainfall depth (mm) in 0.20 mm increments. They are ambient environmental sensors — they do not measure river flow rates, sluice gate positions, or pump run-states. Unheated funnels can also lag during freezing weather or snow.
